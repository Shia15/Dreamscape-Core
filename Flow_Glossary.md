# 🌐 AETHER Flow Glossary
![System Aligned](https://img.shields.io/badge/%E2%9C%85%20System-Aligned%20·%20Up--to--date%20·%20Trusted-brightgreen?style=flat-square)

This glossary defines key symbolic and structural terms used throughout the MARI-Core runtime and interface documentation.

---

## 🔁 Intent  
A structured object derived from user input, representing parsed meaning, tone, and purpose.  
**See also:** [`PromptPersona`](#promptpersona), [`RoutePoint`](#routepoint)

---

## 🧠 CLU (Cognitive Logic Unit)  
Decision engine for routing input, evaluating context, and determining actions.  
**See also:** [`Intent`](#intent), [`RoutePoint`](#routepoint)

---

## 🧳 MemoryEngine  
Handles symbolic short- and long-term memory operations for contextual resonance.  
**See also:** [`EggAgent`](#eggagent)

---

## 💗 EmotionSystem  
Module that evaluates emotional tone and assigns mood tags (e.g., `LOW_ENERGY`).  
**See also:** [`MoodGauge`](#moodgauge), [`PromptPersona`](#promptpersona)

---

## 🥚 EggAgent  
Symbolic entity with archetypal behavior and emotional alignment, used in prompt routing.  
**See also:** [`PromptPersona`](#promptpersona), [`CLU`](#clu-cognitive-logic-unit)

---

## 🌀 MoodGauge  
Tracks and adjusts mood states over time, used for adaptive response modulation.  
**See also:** [`EmotionSystem`](#emotionsystem)

---

## 🔉 InterfaceRenderer  
Outputs final symbolic message or experience (text/audio/visual).  
**See also:** [`ResponseObject`](#responseobject)

---

## 📦 ResponseObject  
Packaged output from the system, containing text, emotion tag, and priority level.  
**See also:** [`InterfaceRenderer`](#interfacerenderer)

---

## 🎭 PromptPersona  
The internal symbolic stance activated based on routing logic and emotional tone.  
Defines the expressive voice of the response (e.g., reflective, directive, nurturing).  
**See also:** [`EmotionSystem`](#emotionsystem), [`EggAgent`](#eggagent), [`Intent`](#intent)

---

## 📍 RoutePoint  
A system-defined emotional or functional endpoint used to direct symbolic flow.  
RoutePoints anchor where in the system a prompt resolves (e.g., agent handoff, memory write).  
**See also:** [`CLU`](#clu-cognitive-logic-unit), [`Intent`](#intent)

---

> This document evolves with the system. Update as new metaphors or modules emerge.
