# ✩ Symbolic Badges

Use these badges in your `README.md`, docs, or onboarding visuals to communicate AETHER’s symbolic logic, development rituals, and agent identity.
Symbolic badges are used to signal the alignment, readiness, and thematic structure of the AETHER system. These visual markers communicate system health, developer rituals, cognitive agent identity, and environment compatibility at a glance.

They serve three key purposes:

✅ Orientation: Help contributors and collaborators immediately understand what parts of the system are active, stable, or in-progress.

🎨 Aesthetic Symbolism: Reinforce the poetic-philosophical foundations of AETHER through color, emoji, and language.

🧭 Functional Transparency: Display which tools, agents, or flows are currently connected or reliable.

Use them in your README.md, developer portals, exhibit indexes, or onboarding materials to tell the story of the codebase symbolically and beautifully.
